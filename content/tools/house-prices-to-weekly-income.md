$$
\text{Price to weekly income}_t = \frac{\text{nominal house price index}_t}{\text{average weekly earnings}_t}
$$

The numerator is the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development nominal house price index for Australia, 2015 = 100. The denominator is Australian Bureau of Statistics average weekly earnings for all employees, in dollars per week. Earnings are half-yearly, so each house price quarter uses the latest published earnings figure.

The level is index points per dollar of weekly earnings, so the absolute value is not a price. Read it against its own history: the shape shows whether housing is stretched relative to the income that services it.

The quartile lines mark the 25th and 75th percentiles of the selected range, so the current reading can be read against its own recent distribution.

**Data Sources**

- Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development, nominal house price index for Australia
- Australian Bureau of Statistics, Average Weekly Earnings, all employees (persons)
- Frequency: quarterly, from 1995
